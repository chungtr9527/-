隱私權政策（繁體中文）

本政策說明《流沙方塊》（Sandslide，以下簡稱「本 App」）如何處理資料。開發者：{{DEVELOPER_NAME}}；聯絡信箱：{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}。

1. 開發者不蒐集你的個人資料

本 App 不需要帳號、不用登入，也沒有自己的伺服器。開發者不會收到你的姓名、電子郵件、位置、聯絡人或任何可以識別你的資料。每日流沙的題目是用日期在你的手機上算出來的，沒有線上排行榜。

2. 只存在你裝置上的資料

為了讓遊戲正常運作，本 App 會把下列資料存在你的裝置裡（Android 用 App 的私有儲存空間，網頁版用瀏覽器的 localStorage）：設定（音效、震動、語言、配色等）、分數與個人紀錄、進行中的存檔、每日流沙的成績與連續天數、本機統計（局數、翻轉次數、最近 5 筆錯誤訊息），以及廣告顯示次數與廣告同意狀態。這些資料不會離開你的裝置，開發者也看不到。解除安裝 App 或清除 App 資料，就會全部刪除。

「分享」功能只會把成績文字或圖片交給系統的分享選單，由你決定要不要傳給誰；本 App 本身不會送出任何資料。

3. 廣告（Google AdMob）

Android 版透過 Google AdMob 顯示廣告：畫面底部的橫幅廣告、結算畫面之後的插頁廣告，以及你主動選擇觀看的獎勵影片（用來救援或補一次翻轉）。廣告由 Google Mobile Ads SDK 提供，該 SDK 會蒐集並與 Google 分享：裝置的廣告 ID、IP 位址（可能被用來推算大概位置）、你與廣告的互動（曝光、點擊）以及診斷資訊（例如當機或效能記錄）。這些資料由 Google 依其隱私權政策處理，用來提供與衡量廣告；開發者只看得到彙總報表，看不到個別使用者的資料。Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站與 App 的資料，請見 policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites。

本 App 把廣告內容分級上限設為「輔導級（PG）」，並未啟用兒童導向處理。網頁版（chungtr9527.github.io/sandslide）沒有任何廣告。

4. 你的選擇

  • 刪除或重設廣告 ID：Android「設定 → Google → 廣告」（或「設定 → 隱私權 → 廣告」）可以「刪除廣告 ID」或「重設廣告 ID」，也可以關閉廣告個人化；刪除後 App 就拿不到廣告 ID。
  • 歐洲經濟區（EEA）與英國使用者：第一次看到結算畫面之前，本 App 會透過 Google 的 User Messaging Platform（UMP）顯示同意對話框，由你決定是否同意個人化廣告。之後隨時可以在 App 的「設定 → 隱私設定」重新開啟對話框修改選擇。
  • 獎勵影片完全自願，不看也能繼續玩；只是沒有救援或額外的翻轉。

5. 兒童

本 App 不是為 13 歲以下兒童設計的，開發者不會刻意蒐集兒童的資料。如果你認為有兒童透過本 App 提供了個人資料，請寫信給我們，我們會協助處理。

6. 資料安全與保留

開發者不保存任何使用者資料，因此沒有可以查詢、更正或刪除的資料庫；上面提到的本機資料完全由你自己掌控。廣告 SDK 的資料傳輸使用加密連線。

7. 政策變更

本政策若有變更，會更新本頁與上方的生效日；重大變更會寫在 App 的更新說明裡。本頁網址：chungtr9527.github.io/-/sandslide/www/privacy.html。

Privacy Policy (English)

This policy explains how Sandslide (流沙方塊, "the app") handles data. Developer: {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}. Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

1. The developer collects no personal data

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. The developer never receives your name, email address, location, contacts or anything else that could identify you. The Daily Slide puzzle is computed from the date on your device; there is no online leaderboard.

2. Data that stays on your device

To work, the app stores the following on your device only (Android: the app's private storage; web: the browser's localStorage): settings (sound, vibration, language, palette), scores and personal records, the saved game in progress, Daily Slide results and streaks, local statistics (games played, tilts, the last 5 error messages), and ad frequency counters and your ad consent status. None of this leaves your device and the developer cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it.

"Share" only hands a score text or image to the system share sheet; you choose who receives it. The app itself sends nothing.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, an interstitial after the results screen, and rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for a rescue or an extra Tilt). Ads are delivered by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which collects and shares with Google: your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which may be used to infer an approximate location), your interactions with ads (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic information (such as crash or performance data). Google processes this data under its own privacy policy to serve and measure ads; the developer only sees aggregated reports, never individual users. See how Google uses data from partner sites and apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The app sets the maximum ad content rating to "PG" and does not enable child-directed treatment. The web version (chungtr9527.github.io/sandslide) shows no ads at all.

4. Your choices

  • Delete or reset your advertising ID: on Android go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID"; you can also opt out of ads personalization. Once deleted, apps can no longer read the ID.
  • Users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom: before the first results screen, the app shows a consent dialog through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), where you decide whether to allow personalised ads. You can reopen it at any time from "Settings → Privacy settings" inside the app.
  • Rewarded videos are always optional; you can keep playing without them, you just won't get the rescue or the extra Tilt.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and the developer does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please email us and we will help.

6. Security and retention

The developer keeps no user data, so there is no database to access, correct or delete; the local data described above is entirely under your control. The ads SDK transmits its data over encrypted connections.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated; material changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes. This page lives at chungtr9527.github.io/-/sandslide/www/privacy.html.
